# Literature caveats for the write-up
<!-- SUMMARY: Source-linked limits on interpreting observed timing as a persistent trait or an intrinsic clock difference -->

The titi multiverse motivates three distinctions in the discussion: dispersion on a given date, repeatability across recording periods, and endogenous circadian timing. In the common-spread model with shared date effects, adding animal-by-fortnight variation lowered the estimated persistent spread from 11.2 to 7.3 minutes. Simulations with temporary offsets alone illustrate how a model can assign short-lived differences to the persistent component. The simulated fortnight duration is a design choice. [Estimates](../analysis/results/titi-multiverse-estimates.csv) · [Simulations](../analysis/results/titi-multiverse-simulations.csv)

- **Refinetti et al. (2016), methods and Figures 3–4.** The ten-day windows quantify differences during the recorded period. A temporary offset can persist throughout such a window. Cross-species comparisons also combine recorded animal locomotor onset with human reports of waking on free days. Use these results as a precedent for comparing observed timing under stated conditions. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2016.04.019)
- **Hozer et al. (2026), discussion and STAR Methods.** Social context shifted observed chimpanzee nesting and waking. The wake model included an individual effect, with usually one animal monitored per night. Tightly clustered behaviour is compatible with individual preferences expressed under shared social constraints. Present the proposed human–chimpanzee evolutionary contrast as a hypothesis. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2025.11.057) · [Code](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Sleep_data_in_wild_chimpanzees/30418156)
- **Samson et al. (2017), §3c and discussion.** Their simulation shows that observed individual timing differences and nocturnal awakenings can account for staggered wakefulness. Temporary differences could also contribute. Actigraphy supplies a proxy for wakefulness, and the evolutionary account remains a separate inference. [Article](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2017.0967)
- **Rhesus macaques.** The controlled studies separately measured free-running period and activity timing under a light schedule. The 2011 age comparison adds four older males and reuses the six young males reported in 2010. Use the paired studies to show that the relation between these measures can vary across samples. [2010 study](https://doi.org/10.1177/0748730410379382) · [2011 study](https://doi.org/10.1177/0748730410395849)

Loftus and Fele provide direct evidence of coordination in observed baboon behaviour. Their studies motivate comparing repeatable differences in sleep onset and final waking within the same animals and nights. Keep that comparison focused on behaviour under observed conditions. [Loftus et al.](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73695) · [Fele et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2025.10.028)
